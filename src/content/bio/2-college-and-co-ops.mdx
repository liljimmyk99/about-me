At Penn State I studied Human-Centered Design & Development, a degree that sits between design thinking and software development. I graduated magna cum laude in 2022.

Outside the classroom I was a peer tutor for three years and a tutoring co-coordinator. In the summer of 2021 I interned with Ford's Emerging Technologies and Innovation team, helping teams put Design Thinking into practice. Two intern innovation challenge teams I worked on finished as "Most Viable" and as a finalist. That summer I was also a Margaret Hamilton Distinguished Intern with Penn State Enterprise IT.
