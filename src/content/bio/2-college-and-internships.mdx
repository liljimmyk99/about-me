At Penn State, I earned my degree in Human-Centered Design & Development (HCDD)—a hybrid discipline bridging software engineering with user-centered design. To reinforce core concepts, I served as a Learning Assistant and Peer Tutor, eventually advancing to Co-Coordinator for three years to lead program operations, manage peer tutoring, and facilitate technical workshops.

As a Margaret Hamilton Distinguished Intern with Penn State Enterprise IT, I gained hands-on experience with production coding standards and patterns. Collaborating with a senior engineer and fellow intern, I architected and deployed a custom AWS-powered Alexa Skill from scratch, delivering real-time university dining menus and academic calendar integration to campus users.

I later joined Ford’s Emerging Technologies & Innovation team as an intern, shifting focus from core software development to design strategy. In this role, I coached internal cross-functional engineering teams on applying Design Thinking principles, running interactive workshops, and guiding teams through complex, real-world product challenges.
