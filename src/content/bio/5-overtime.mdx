I'm a Senior Instructor at Action Karate, where I teach American Kempo to students from age three to adult. I earned my third-degree black belt, and the rank of Sensei, in 2023.

Since 2023 I've been a volunteer coordinator for the FIRST district event Team 5401 hosts in Bensalem, and I referee an event at Centennial High School. At Calvary Chapel of Philadelphia I serve in the Koinonia Café, a habit that started at the East Coast Pastors conference in June 2024, and I lead a discipleship group of Senior High boys. On game days I'm on the concierge staff at Lincoln Financial Field.
