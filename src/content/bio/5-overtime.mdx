From 2022 to 2024 I was a Senior Instructor at Action Karate, first in Drexel Hill and then at a second location in Hamilton, teaching American Kempo to students from age three to adult. I earned my third-degree black belt, and the rank of Sensei, in 2023.

Since 2023 I've been the volunteer coordinator for the FIRST district event Team 5401 hosts in Bensalem, and I referee an event at Centennial High School. Before that I mentored the team's programmers through April 2024, and I was elected by my peers to co-lead Ford's Enterprise Technology College Graduate program of more than 250 members, after a year as its finance lead.

At Calvary Chapel of Philadelphia I serve in the Koinonia Café, a habit that started at the East Coast Pastors conference in June 2024. Since September 2025 I've also led a discipleship group of Senior High boys. On Eagles game days I'm on the concierge staff at Lincoln Financial Field working and cheering for my favorite sports team.
