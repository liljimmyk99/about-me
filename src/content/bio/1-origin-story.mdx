I was born and raised in Bensalem, Pennsylvania, and I still live there. Most of what I do outside of work comes from the same idea: the community around me helped me grow up, and I want to return the favor.

At Bensalem High School I helped found the Fightin' Robotic Owls, FIRST Robotics Team 5401, and went on to become its program manager. That was my first taste of leading a group toward a shared goal, and I'm still mentoring the team's programmers today.
