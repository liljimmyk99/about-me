In September 2022 I joined Ford through the Ford College Graduate (FCG) program as a SYNC4 software developer. I built React apps that run on custom in-vehicle hardware for the Mustang Mach-E and the F-150 Lightning, including charging and backup power apps and games, and wrote and interpreted the high-level designs for new games.

In 2024 I moved to Ford Credit's mobile platform team. For a year I was both a mobile platform engineer and the team's technical project manager where I helped migrate the organization from Rally to Jira and established standards we would follow to this day. As a developer, I built a SwiftUI design system from scratch, maintained our deployment pipelines in BitRise, facilitated every Scrum ceremony and led the move from Rally to Jira and from WebEx to MS Teams. 

In 2025, our platform and feature teams combined and I became an iOS software engineer full-time, working on the Ford Credit Mobile Application. I develop, maintain and refactor the app, and I still step in as the team's TPM for Scrum ceremonies when needed.

Much of what I do now is figuring out how AI fits into a real codebase. I wrote our Development Style Guide along with AI instructions, so pull requests are reviewed against the same standard I wrote down. From there I use AI to refactor existing code to conform to the guide, migrate legacy XCTests to the new Swift Testing framework, and generate a mocked data layer for UI testing. The goal is to let the tools handle the tedious parts so the team can spend its time on the decisions that need a person.
