In September 2022 I joined Ford as a SYNC4 software developer. I built React apps that run on custom in-vehicle hardware for the Mustang Mach-E and the F-150 Lightning, including charging and backup power apps and games, and wrote and interpreted the high-level designs for new features.

In 2024 I moved to Ford Credit's mobile platform team. For a year I was both a mobile platform engineer and the team's technical project manager. I built a SwiftUI design system from scratch, maintained our deployment pipelines, facilitated every Scrum ceremony and led the move from Rally to Jira and from WebEx to MS Teams. In 2025 I became an iOS software engineer full time.
