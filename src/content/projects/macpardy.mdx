## The pregame

A trivia night shouldn't need accounts, a server or an internet connection. Macpardy runs the whole game from one Mac: the host drives a control window, the room watches a full-screen display on a TV or projector, and teams buzz in from their phones over the local network.

## Why did I create this?
The Jeopardy-like games are either a powerpoint template or a website.  I don't pay for Office-products on my personal machine and the area where I want to play this trivia game does not have reliable internet.  I wanted a self contained app that has keyboard short cuts that make sense that contains native picture support and looks sleek.  Additionally, I wanted to experiment with using AI agents to bring an idea to life rather than doing this by hand.

## The playbook

- Build a game in the editor (6 × 5 boards for two rounds, plus Final Macpardy) with Daily Doubles and optional pictures on clues, or import one from a CSV template.
- Separate host and audience windows, with a timer, scores, Daily Double and Final wagers.
- Phone buzzers: scan a QR code, pick a team, buzz in. Fair tie-breaking, a 5 second response timer and wrong-answer lockout.
- Display animations for the board intro, clue flips and score changes, which can be switched off in Settings.
- Auto-save, so a game can be resumed after a quit or crash.

## My role

Solo project: product design, the SwiftUI app, the phone buzzer page and the Tuist project tooling.
