## The pregame

I like to track my spending by hand so I know where every dollar goes. For years that meant a spreadsheet. I wanted something faster for entering transactions and income, and an application can do far more than a sheet can. There's no bank syncing here, on purpose: the point is the habit of recording.

## The playbook

- Record monthly income by source, with gross, gross taxable and net amounts, and every expense by category.
- View totals and trends by month and year.
- One self-hosted Go API with a SQLite database, described by an OpenAPI spec that both clients build against.
- A React web app and a native SwiftUI Mac app on top of that one API.
- All money is stored as integer cents, never floats, and the tax rate is derived from the amounts instead of stored.

## My role

Solo project: product requirements, the OpenAPI contract, the Go backend and both clients.

## Film review

This is the second pass at the idea. My first attempt, a SwiftUI Mac app, got through its whole minimum viable product but needed a rewrite. Starting over with a written contract (the OpenAPI spec first, then the backend, then the clients) is what makes building two clients on one API manageable.
